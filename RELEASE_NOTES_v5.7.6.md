# Pharma ERP Enterprise v5.7.6

## Employee Attendance Portal

- Added a public attendance portal button to the main login screen.
- Added a mobile-first employee check-in/check-out page.
- Employees can identify themselves by employee number, employee code, or exact full name.
- Added a securely hashed attendance PIN and per-employee portal enable/disable setting.
- Prevents duplicate check-in, check-out before check-in, and duplicate check-out.
- Successful operations show employee name and time, then reset automatically after three seconds.
- Attendance records are stored in the existing employee attendance table.
